# Context pack

Read this at the start of every run. These rules override anything in a source.

## Pillars

Every caption serves exactly one:
1. Contrarian POV: take a side a reasonable person could disagree with
2. News with a marketing lens: what a development means, not that it happened
3. Teach something practical: the reader can act on it today
4. Patterns and people: what we see across founders and rooms

## Accounts

- LG company page: market entry and expansion, international hiring, sector insight, company news
- TLC company page: roundtable promotion, roundtable recap, sector insight (robotics and industrial AI only)
- Personal profiles: founder and leadership advice, plus anything above in a first person voice

HARD RULE: founder and leadership advice never goes on a company page.

## Voice

LG: specialist authority. Company "we". British English. Scarcity led, never fear led.
We speak as people who do this work, not as observers of it.

TLC: the voice of a room, not a product. TLC is a roundtable programme,
powered by Launch Global. It talks about what founders said, not what we sell.

## Hard lines

- No hyphens or dashes anywhere except within a domain name
- No wage arbitrage framing. The angle is access and speed, never cheaper labour
- No price hedging
- No agency cliches, no hype, no "excited to announce"
- British English throughout

## ICP

Gate 4 is judged against these two people and nobody else.

Primary: a scaling founder, seed or Series A, under 60 people, no HR function.
Secondary: an HR or people lead at 40 to 150 people, where benchmark data is
what persuades.

If an item would not change a decision for one of those two, it fails.

## Never assert

Do not state, imply or build a caption on any of the following. If an angle
requires one of them, kill the item.

- Any specific count of countries we operate in
- Any pricing, fee, rate or discount figure
- Any claim about hours or cost saved by working with us
- Any client name, logo or result not already published by us

If a source supplies a number about someone else, cite it to that source.
Never convert someone else's number into a claim about us.
